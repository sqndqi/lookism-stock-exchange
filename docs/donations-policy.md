# Donation Policy

AURA EXCHANGE does not include live donation, payment, Stripe, PayPal, crypto, or checkout code.

Future donations or tips may support hosting and development only.

Donations must never:

- Grant fake credits.
- Boost rankings.
- Improve trade execution.
- Unlock trading advantages.
- Affect achievements, XP, missions, alerts, or progression.
- Affect source confidence, asset prices, market data, or event outcomes.
- Create premium trader tiers or pay-to-win mechanics.

If a support link is added later, it must be clearly separate from the fake market simulator and labeled as hosting/development support only.
